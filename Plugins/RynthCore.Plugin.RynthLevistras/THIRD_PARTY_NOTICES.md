# Third-party notices: RynthLevistras

RynthLevistras's quest pack (`Resources/quests.levistras.csv`, offered to RynthOracle's Quests tab
through "RynthServer.QuestPack") is **Oracle of Dereth**'s Levistras rows: the master quest list of
that Decal plugin for Asheron's Call by **Advis Eveldan** (GitHub:
[advis61](https://github.com/advis61/OracleOfDereth)), split by its own Server column. The rows
were part of RynthOracle until 2026-10-21 and moved here, with the server they belong to.
Thank you, Advis!
